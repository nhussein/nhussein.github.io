I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at Michigan State University, working in the SPriNT-AI Lab under Prof. Karthik Nandakumar and Prof. Anil K. Jain. My research focuses on Trustworthy AI and Biometrics — building AI systems that are certified, robust, and deployable in safety-critical domains such as biometric identification and medical imaging.

Previously, I completed an MSc in Machine Learning at MBZUAI on a full scholarship, where my thesis focused on certifying the robustness of medical Vision-Language Models via prompt learning. I hold a BSc in Applied Mathematics and Statistics from Khalifa University (full scholarship, UAE Ministry of Education), graduating with Excellent and Highest Honors.

I have published at NeurIPS, MICCAI, ICLR, IJCB, ICIP, and CVPR/ICML workshops, and am the recipient of the Best Poster Award at CITeR 2026, 1st place at NeurIPS 2024 ETI Challenge, and the UAE Golden Visa.

Research Focus

Biometrics

Diffusion-based generation of fingerprint, palmprint, and latent biometric images — studying identity preservation, intra-class variability, and minutiae conditioning.

Trustworthy AI

Building AI systems that are certified, robust, and reliable — spanning certified robustness, watermarking for generative models, and adversarial defenses.
