hussei52@msu.edu Scholar LinkedIn GitHub

I am a PhD student in CSE at Michigan State University, advised by Prof. Karthik Nandakumar and Prof. Anil K. Jain in the SPriNT-AI Lab. My research focuses on Trustworthy AI and Biometrics.

Previously, I completed an MSc in Machine Learning at MBZUAI (full scholarship), where my thesis focused on certifying robustness of medical Vision-Language Models via prompt learning. I hold a BSc in Applied Mathematics and Statistics from Khalifa University (full scholarship, UAE Ministry of Education), graduating with Excellent and Highest Honors.
